# Scheme Baseline — Vidiyal Payanam → Magalir Vetri Payanam

`[VETRI] [VIDIYAL] [TNSTC] [MTC] [SETC]` — 2026-10-03. Anchor note: everything in the hub hangs off these sourced numbers.

## 1. Genealogy

| Date | Event | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| May 2021 | **Vidiyal Payanam** launched by DMK govt: free bus travel for women in ordinary city/town buses of 7 State Transport Corporations (STCs); no income limit; photo-ID based; no ticket issued | [^onmanorama-diff] |
| May 2026 (approx.) | TVK manifesto promise "Vetri Payanam" — free travel for women in **all** government buses, surfaced during campaign & government formation | [^onmanorama-diff] |
| 24 Aug 2026 | CM C. Joseph Vijay announces **Vetri Payanam** expansion in Assembly under **Rule 110**: effective 2 Oct 2026, est. ₹6,000 cr/yr; extends to transgender persons | [^indiasnews] |
| 21 Sep 2026 | CM reviews launch readiness at Secretariat: ETM linking complete, testing by 25 Sep, trial run 27 Sep | [^indiatoday] |
| 2 Oct 2026 | **Statewide launch** at Koyambedu (Gandhi Jayanti); CM boards Chennai–Kancheepuram 76B, issues first free tickets; parallel launch event at Ambattur; scheme branded **Magalir Vetri Payanam** | [^thehindu] [^etv] [^ani] [^southfirst] |

## 2. What Vetri Payanam changed vs Vidiyal Payanam

| Dimension | Vidiyal (2021) | Vetri (Oct 2026) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ordinary city/town buses | Free | Free (continues) |
| Express / LSS / Deluxe | Paid | **Free** within corporation & municipal limits [^indiatoday] |
| Mofussil Ordinary (inter-district) | Paid | **Free** [^southfirst] |
| Ghat Ordinary (hill routes) | Free only up to 40 km | **Free beyond 40 km** [^govtschemes-vetri] |
| Transgender persons | In scheme since 2021 per some reports; explicitly restated | Explicitly included in expansion [^jagranjosh] |
| Ticketing | No ticket issued | ETM-linked designated buses; conductor issues zero-fare ticket [^etv] |

## 3. Key numbers (launch scale)

- **12,692** designated buses of **18,413** scheduled buses across 7 STCs = **69%** of fleet [^indiatoday] [^newkerala]
- **1,65,312** daily single trips under the scheme (reported as 85% of total operations; pre-expansion total given as 94,649 singles — the two figures don't reconcile arithmetically; flag for verification against ETM data) [^newkerala]
- **~84.32 lakh** women + trans riders/day projected [^indiatoday]
- **~₹6,000 crore/yr** estimated expenditure (Assembly statement, 24 Aug 2026) [^indiasnews]
- Legacy baseline: **~70 lakh** women/day rode free under Vidiyal on **7,331** ordinary buses (Transport Dept figures, 2026) [^indiancommunity]
- STC context: combined daily operational loss ~**₹19 crore**; diesel price hike adds **₹175.58 cr/yr**; last fare revision Jan 2018 [^indiancommunity]
