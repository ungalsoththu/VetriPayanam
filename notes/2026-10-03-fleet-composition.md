## Service-class rules (Daily Thanthi 2026-10-01)

- Express routes: free up to **100 km**
- LSS: free up to **40 km**
- Deluxe: free up to **50 km**
- Mofussil Ordinary: free (ordinary fare class)
- Ghat Ordinary: free on routes **>40 km**
- Excluded: A/C buses, Volvo, SETC long-distance, TNSTC Express >100 km legs
- Eligible buses marked with stickers bearing CM photo; no income limit, no journey cap

## Chain-travel mechanics (DT 2026-10-01)

Free long-distance travel possible by hopping eligible buses: Chennai → Kanyakumari via Chengalpattu, Tindivanam, Villupuram, Vriddhachalam, Trichy, Dindigul, Madurai, Virudhunagar, Kovilpatti, Tirunelveli, Nagercoil. DT cites live corridors: Trichy–Dindigul, Thanjavur–Nagapattinam/Velankanni, Coimbatore–Palani/Erode, Erode–Salem, Madurai–Virudhunagar–Kovilpatti, Tirunelveli–Nagercoil. Time-cost is the hidden price — each hop waits for the next bus.
